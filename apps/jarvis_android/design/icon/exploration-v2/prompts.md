# 图标第二轮概念提示词

使用内置 image_gen，未使用 CLI/API fallback。

## 01 连续曲线探索（未采纳）

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: one premium mobile app icon concept for JARVIS, an intelligent sci-fi personal assistant. Square 1024x1024, pure BLACK background filling the full square, no rounded-square frame, ONE centered original WHITE symbol, generously spaced, symbol occupies 54-60% of square. This is a FLAT VECTOR-STYLE brand identity, not an illustration. The user wants the elegant instant recognition of leading minimal consumer AI app marks but an original identity. Design a single beautiful continuous fluid silhouette: one sweeping, tapered, asymmetric white ribbon looping around a small tilted teardrop of negative black space. The loop should suggest a perceptive, curious intelligence turning its attention toward the upper right, with an elegant open tail and a little forward momentum. Let a subtle hidden J-like gesture emerge from its lower curve without drawing a letter or adding text. The mark should feel alive and calm, not industrial, not cute. Science fiction comes from extraordinary curve precision, optical balance, intelligent negative space and controlled asymmetry. Use one coherent flowing shape, clean thick-to-thin contour, substantial visual weight, carefully shaped void; strongly legible at 32px. ONE solid white color, no shading, no gradients, no glow, no outlines, no small details. Avoid a sphere/planet, orbits, stars/sparkles, atom diagrams, mechanical brackets, robot faces, visor masks, eyes with eyelashes, generic four-point AI stars, hexagons, disconnected geometric pieces, generic infinity sign, letter monograms, any imitation of an existing logo. No text, no wordmark, no typography, no multiple options, no mockup, no phone, no extra objects. Finished elegant original brand mark for a fluid artificial intelligence, high-end graphic design, strictly simple black-and-white graphic.

## 02 感知核心（供评图）

Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE finished premium minimal original app icon concept for JARVIS, a perceptive, fluid sci-fi AI companion. Pure black square background, centered elegant WHITE graphic occupying about 55% of the square with generous margins. Flat high-contrast vector-style design, beautifully precise curves, no texture or effects. The concept is a living aperture / intelligent consciousness: THREE compact, softly tapered flowing blades subtly curling around ONE small tilted almond-shaped negative-space opening. Their movement should converge into a calm attentive focus angled a little up and right. Three blades should visually merge into one coherent poised silhouette, not look like disconnected industrial parts. Organic thick-to-thin curves, subtle controlled asymmetry, elegant internal spacing, neither a perfect geometric pinwheel nor a fidget spinner. Emphasize a compact memorable icon with the simplicity and optical authority of leading consumer AI brands, but genuinely original. Make it feel intelligent, alive and futuristic through its contour alone, not through decorations. Solid white only, black background. No letters or monograms, no long swoosh tails, no horns, no literal human or robot eye, no visor/mask, no face, no petals or leaf/eco logo, no flame, no planet, no orbital rings, no atoms, no sparkle/star symbol, no hexagon, no mechanical brackets, no infinity sign, no gradients, no glow, no 3D, no shadows, no little dots, no extra border or frame, no text or multiple options. Simple enough to reproduce perfectly as an SVG and immediately recognizable at 32px. High-end iconic brand mark, evocative of focused artificial awareness.
